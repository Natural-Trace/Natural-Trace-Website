Header images for Insights articles land here when they are uploaded through the
admin panel. Nothing else uses this folder.

Landscape, around 1200px wide, is the right shape: the image runs full width at
the top of the article and is cropped into the card on the Insights index.

Large photographs should be saved as WebP or JPEG rather than PNG. A PNG
photograph is often ten times the size for no visible difference, and every
visitor downloads it.
