    How a result is reached

    Three things are scored low, medium or high: the maximum processing temperature, the pH, and the hardest of the formats selected.

    • Any high, or two or more mediums → Testing required
    • Exactly one medium → Likely compatible
    • No mediums and no highs → Compatible

    "Not sure" is scored as medium for both temperature and pH.

    Then tested evidence is applied: a recorded pass in the same category and format lifts "compatible with conditions" to "compatible", and a recorded failure drops any result to "testing required".
