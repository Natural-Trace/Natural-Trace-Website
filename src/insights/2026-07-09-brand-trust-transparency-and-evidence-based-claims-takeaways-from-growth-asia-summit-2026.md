## Claims Need Evidence

The Growth Asia Summit 2026 opened on a theme that came back in session after session: consumers expect brands to back their product claims with evidence.

For nutraceutical, supplement and functional food brands, that means proof of where an ingredient came from and that it is what the label says.

## What This Means for the Industry

- Transparency is now the baseline. Consumers expect to see where ingredients come from and how products are made.
- Claims backed by traceable, auditable data are becoming how brands set themselves apart.
- Verification is moving to batch level. Brands that can prove origin batch by batch have an edge with regulators and with consumers.
