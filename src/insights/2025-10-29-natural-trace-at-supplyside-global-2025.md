<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Natural Trace, an award-winning biotech ingredient company pioneering food-safe traceability, will showcase its latest in-product tagging technology at SupplySide Global 2025, Booth #921. The company’s breakthrough platform is redefining how nutraceutical and ingredient brands verify authenticity, protect intellectual property, and strengthen consumer trust across complex global supply chains.</span></p>
<h3><b>Bringing authenticity to life</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">As brands face growing risks of ingredient fraud, mislabelling, and counterfeiting, Natural Trace is introducing a new era of transparency, one that starts within the product itself.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At the heart of the platform is NaturalTag™, a GMO-Free, food-grade ingredient tracer that’s added directly into raw materials or finished products, creating an </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">invisible fingerprint</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that securely travels through every stage of production and distribution.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Supported by NaturalDetect™, a highly sensitive real-time PCR-based testing method with proprietary knowhow, and NaturalCloud™, a digital traceability platform, Natural Trace provides verifiable, batch-level insights into product provenance. Together, these three integrated technologies give companies full control and visibility across their supply chain.</span></p>
<h3><b>Detecting dilution and protecting proprietary formulations</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">One of the most powerful capabilities of Natural Trace’s platform is its ability to detect dilution or adulteration of proprietary ingredients, a growing issue in premium nutraceuticals. By embedding NaturalTag™ directly within the ingredient, reduction in concentration or unauthorised blending may be identified through precise PCR quantification via NaturalDetect™.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This enables brands to:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Verify full-strength formulations and guard against cost-cutting dilution</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Confirm licensed ingredient usage by third-party manufacturers or co-packers</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Protect the commercial value of patented or branded formulations</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Provide forensic-level evidence in cases of mislabelling or adulteration</span>&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For ingredient brands and suppliers, this offers a new layer of protection transforming proprietary formulas into verifiable assets with measurable integrity.</span></p>
<h3><b>A new era for transparency and trust</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">“Today’s nutraceutical market is driven by trust,” said Dr. Julia Lee, CEO of Natural Trace. “Manufacturing partners expect more than just paperwork; they want proof. Our technology provides that proof, helping brands authenticate products in real time, safeguard their reputation, and demonstrate integrity from source to shelf.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Natural Trace’s in-product tagging is compatible with a wide range of applications, from botanical extracts and dietary supplements to finished formulations and powders. Because the tag is inert, tasteless, and food-safe, it can be easily and  seamlessly integrated without affecting product quality or sensory properties.</span></p>
<h3><b>Benefits for nutraceutical innovators and ingredient suppliers</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">At SupplySide Global, Natural Trace will highlight how its platform helps:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Protect branded ingredients and formulations</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> from counterfeiting or dilution</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Enable verification</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> of authenticity anywhere along the supply chain</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Deliver digital proof of provenance</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> through cloud-connected data insights</span>&nbsp;</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Build trade partner confidence</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> through verifiable “source-to-capsule” integrity</span>&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<h3><b>Driving innovation across the industry</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Following successful customer use-cases and pilot programs with nutraceutical and functional food partners, Natural Trace continues to expand globally, partnering with ingredient manufacturers, CDMOs, and finished product brands to integrate in-product traceability at scale.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The company’s mission is clear: become the leading provider for in-product traceability solution globally, preventing fraud, and enhancing consumer trust in the global food supply system.</span></p>
