Natural Trace has been named a 2026 Emerging Champion at the IP for Growth Awards, presented during Singapore IP Week.

For Natural Trace, intellectual property sits at the heart of what we are building.

Our technology is designed to give food and nutraceutical ingredients their own unique, verifiable identity within the product itself, helping companies protect differentiated ingredients, verify supply chains and strengthen confidence in the products they bring to market.

A special mention goes to our Co-CEO Alrik Tan, who has led Natural Trace's IP strategy and played a key role in building and protecting the intellectual property behind our technology.

To have that innovation recognised alongside companies from across the region is a milestone for our whole team.

Our thanks to the Intellectual Property Office of Singapore (IPOS) and the organisers of the IP for Growth Awards.
